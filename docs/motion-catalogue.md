## Motion catalogue (applies to every screen)

Tokens live in `design/tokens/tokens.json` (`motion`). Reduced motion = short cross-fade, no movement.

| Moment | Motion |
|---|---|
| App open | Greeting fades up, sections stagger in 40 ms apart, in the order they sit (Calm Today: Up next, then the timeline, then the moment cards — brief, review, shutdown, update — then Needs you, Anytime today and done today; core `TodayOrderRules`) |
| Theme change | Every colour blends across (`themeBlend`) |

| Up next changes | Card content cross-slides; old item settles into the list. Reduced motion: cross-fades |
