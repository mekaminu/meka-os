what: the headlines on the topics you choose, also read in the morning brief and shown on the news widget.
how: Open News to choose topics and sources; say "the headlines" or "more news" to Talk to hear them.
tip: A brass dot marks a story you haven't read; opening it (here or on the other app) marks it read, and Mark all read puts the rest away, with Undo. Save for the train in a story puts its link in your train queue. Under Topics, Sources has a switch for each publisher: switch one off and its stories leave News, the ticker and the brief on both apps.
