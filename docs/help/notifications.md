what: what reaches you and when: decisions and urgent things come at once, most things wait for the midday and evening digests, nothing in quiet hours.
how: Set quiet hours and the digest times there, and tap Send a test to check both the phone's own path and the server's push.
tip: Quiet between digests is normal.
tip: Each category can be turned down in Android's own settings: Health → Notifications shows which are off.
