what: your spending by category, month by month, from the statements of your bank and card accounts, kept behind your fingerprint (Touch ID on the Mac). Read-only: Navilife never moves money, pays anything or keeps a bank password, and nothing it shows is financial advice.
how: Download a statement from your bank's website or app as CSV (some call it a spreadsheet or Excel file). Open Ask → Life → Money, confirm it's you, go to Accounts and tap Import, export and delete (under MORE), type which account it is (e.g. Current account) and tap Import a statement file, then pick the file. Importing the same months again adds nothing twice.
tip: To add a statement to an account already there, tap it under ACCOUNTS and choose Import a statement into it.
mac_how: Open Ask → Life → Money, confirm with Touch ID, go to Accounts and click Import, export and delete (under MORE), type which account it is and click Import a statement file…, then choose the CSV file you downloaded from your bank (or click an account under ACCOUNTS → Import a statement into it…).
tip: Each month shows what you spent, what came in, and each category with its share of the month; for a finished month it says how that compares with your average over the 6 months before, and lights a category that's well over it. Use ‹ and › to go back a month or forward again.
tip: A payment in the wrong category? Tap it (click on the Mac): its sheet shows the payee and your categories as chips, the ones you use most first. Pick the right one and every payment from that payee moves there, past and future ("Moved 14 payments from … to …", with Undo), on the Fold and the Mac alike. Need, Shared, Always shared and Side hustle sit below; Done closes it.
tip: Lots of payments in Other? Under Spending's categories, "52 payments without a category · Sort them" opens a quick sorter: one payee at a time, the biggest first. Tap a category and every payment from that payee moves there, past and future (Undo under the chips), and the next payee comes up at once; Skip leaves one for later, + New category makes one of your own. The same on the Mac.
tip: On Spending, the chips over the category rows (beside All) switch between Categories and Needs vs wants, which splits the month in two (groceries, bills, transport, fuel, home, health, the kids and fees are needs; the rest wants) and names each side's biggest categories.
tip: A payee in the wrong half? Tap one of their payments and switch Need on or off: every payment to them moves, past and future (with Undo), on the Fold and the Mac alike. No total changes.
tip: Want a category of your own (Vape, Dog, Hobbies)? Tap a payment, then + New category at the end of the chips: type its name, pick a colour and an icon, and tap Create. That payee's payments move into it, past and future ("Made Vape · moved 14 payments from …", with Undo), it gets its own row and slice on Spending, and its chip comes first next time, on the Fold and the Mac alike.
tip: Your own categories live in Money → Accounts under YOUR CATEGORIES. Tap one to rename it or change its colour and icon (Save), Hide from the ring (its slice turns grey like Other, its row stays), merge it into another category (its payees move there, past and future), or Delete category: it asks first, then its payees go to Other. Every change has Undo and shows on the Fold and the Mac alike.
tip: A monthly budget on a category of yours? Money → Accounts → YOUR CATEGORIES, tap it and type an amount under MONTHLY CAP (e.g. £60): its sheet and row show this month against it ("£17.50 of £20 · £2.50 left"), and it joins CHALLENGES and Goals' streaks. "Vape £60" in the challenges' cap field works too. Remove takes it off, with Undo, on the Fold and the Mac.
tip: A card statement that lists purchases as positive amounts is read the right way round (purchases as money out); the line after an import says so.
tip: Money → Accounts lists each account once, by a short name ("HSBC current"), with its balance (a card's as owed) or Add balance. Tap one for its sheet: give it your own name under Name ("Joint bills"; clear it for its own name), type its balance, switch It's a card or Side hustle account, or import a statement into it. Your name for it shows on Accounts, Bills and card reminders.
tip: Remove account in an account's sheet asks first, then takes it and its transactions off every device; Delete all money data (Accounts → MORE → Import, export and delete) asks first, then clears everything. Your bank keeps its own records.
tip: From your banks: your accounts and cards fill Money by themselves through Lunch Flow, read-only, every few hours. Refresh fetches them now (it takes a minute), and so does the Updated pill beside the title; the line says when they were last fetched and how many accounts.
