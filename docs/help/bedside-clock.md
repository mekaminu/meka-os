what: a calm night clock with the date, the watch face, the next alarm and the evening's lines, for the Fold half folded on the bedside table.
how: Open Navilife on the inner screen, turn the phone landscape and half fold it like a laptop on the table: the clock comes up by itself within a couple of seconds. Or open it any time from Ask → Daily → Bedside clock, or say "bedside clock" to Talk; ‹ Back leaves it.
tip: The Fold 8 has no Flex mode panel in Samsung Labs, and Navilife doesn't need one: it reads the hinge itself.
tip: Keep auto-rotate on, or the landscape window never comes.
tip: If it still doesn't appear, Ask → Settings → Health → Fold shows what the phone reported.
