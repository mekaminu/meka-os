what: whether the AI is on and what it has cost this month against its budget.
how: When it's off or the budget is used, Ask's field only searches and answers come from the device.
tip: Tap Navilife's AI in Settings to unfold this month's spend by feature (Talk, Ask, Mail, Messages…), the costliest first, each with its calls and cost.
tip: If Anthropic refuses the key (revoked or expired), the row and Health turn red: make a new key in Anthropic's Console and paste it into Secrets Manager; nothing else changes.
tip: While the AI is off, over budget or can't be reached, Settings lists what each AI feature does instead (the brief is read from your day as it is, voice notes keep their words, replies aren't drafted), and the brief and From your mail say so in one line.
tip: Navilife's own voice (Amazon Polly) is counted in the same budget at Polly's list price, shown as Voice with its clips; once the month's budget is used, the phone's or Mac's own voice speaks until the 1st. Google's natural voices stay inside their free allowance, so they cost nothing.
