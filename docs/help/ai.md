what: whether the AI is on and what it has cost this month against its budget.
how: When it's off or the budget is used, Ask's field only searches and answers come from the device.
tip: Tap Navilife's AI in Settings to unfold this month's spend by feature (Talk, Ask, Mail, Messages…), the costliest first, each with its calls and cost.
tip: If Anthropic refuses the key (revoked or expired), the row and Health turn red: make a new key in Anthropic's Console and paste it into Secrets Manager; nothing else changes.
tip: While the AI is off, over budget or can't be reached, Settings lists what each AI feature does instead (the brief is read from your day as it is, voice notes keep their words, replies aren't drafted), and the brief and From your mail say so in one line.
tip: Navilife's own voice (Amazon Polly) is counted in the same budget at Polly's list price, shown as Voice with its clips; once the month's budget is used, the phone's or Mac's own voice speaks until the 1st. Google's natural voices stay inside their free allowance, so they cost nothing.
tip: Ask or Talk about something current (the news, a score, a price, the weather somewhere else) and Navilife looks it up on the web with Brave Search and shows the source under the answer, a tap opens it; only the question's words are searched, never anything about your own life, and at most 40 a day.
tip: Mention something lasting in conversation (a size, the plumber, the GP) and Navilife offers once to keep it on What Navilife knows; nothing is kept until you tap.
tip: Ask or Talk about another day ("what's on Thursday?", "am I free this weekend?", "what have I got next week?", "what did I do yesterday?") and Navilife looks those days up in your calendar and tasks on your device, up to a week at a time, and can still offer to move what it finds; your calendar is read 30 days ahead, so a day further off shows only its tasks.
tip: Ask about a sender ("did anything come from Amazon?", "have I heard from the school?") and Navilife also looks through what its autopilot tidied from them in the last 14 days, on your device, and says what it was sorted as and where it is now: the archive, Trash or back in the inbox.
tip: Ask about trains on another day ("train or drive to the match on Saturday?", "any engineering works tomorrow?") and Navilife says whether it's an office day, your route and TfL's planned works for that morning once it has read them (from 17:00 the evening before). Live train times only show for the next couple of hours.
