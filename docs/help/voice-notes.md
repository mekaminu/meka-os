what: talk a rambling note and Navilife turns it into words on the phone or the Mac, then offers the tasks, dates and reminders in it as cards for you to add. The recording is kept for 30 days.
how: Open Capture (the widget, the quick-settings tile or Share) and hold the 🎙: it records until you tap Done (Pause stops it for a moment, five minutes at most). Or say "take a voice note" to Talk. When you stop, the words appear and Navilife reads them: Add the cards you want; anything you leave waits in Needs you.
mac_how: On the Mac hold the mic at the end of Today's capture field (a click still talks), or say "take a voice note" to Talk: the Mac records it and turns it into words itself, keeps it encrypted on the Mac for 30 days, and its cards wait in Needs you.
tip: The recording and its words stay on the device that made it (the phone or the Mac), encrypted, and go after 30 days; tap Keep on a note in Ask → Daily → Voice notes to hold on to it. Only the words, never the recording, go to Navilife's AI.
tip: Turning speech into words happens on the device: Android's own recogniser on the phone (Android 13 or later), Dictation's English on the Mac. If it can't, the recording is still kept and you can play it back.
tip: No signal? The note is still recorded and turned into words; tap Make cards on it in Voice notes once you're back online.
tip: Navilife's AI tidies the note into a few short lines and says who it mentions; your own words stay under "Your words". Ideas with no date can go to Lists → Someday → Ideas with one tap.
tip: A quick tap on the 🎙 is still the short way: say one thing and it fills the capture field.
