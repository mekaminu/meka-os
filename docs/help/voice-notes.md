what: talk a rambling note and Navilife turns it into words on the phone, then offers the tasks, dates and reminders in it as cards for you to add. The recording is kept for 30 days.
how: Open Capture (the widget, the quick-settings tile or Share) and hold the 🎙: it records until you tap Done (Pause stops it for a moment, five minutes at most). Or say "take a voice note" to Talk. When you stop, the words appear and Navilife reads them: Add the cards you want; anything you leave waits in Needs you.
tip: The recording and its words stay on the phone, encrypted, and go after 30 days; tap Keep on a note in Ask → Daily → Voice notes to hold on to it. Only the words, never the recording, go to Navilife's AI.
tip: Turning speech into words happens on the phone with Android's own recogniser (Android 13 or later). If it can't, the recording is still kept and you can play it back.
tip: No signal? The note is still recorded and turned into words; tap Make cards on it in Voice notes once you're back online.
tip: A quick tap on the 🎙 is still the short way: say one thing and it fills the capture field.
