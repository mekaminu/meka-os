what: the errands you asked Obi to look into, and the things he keeps an eye on for you: renewals coming up, birthdays in the next ten days, things you're waiting on, what he tidied in your mail today and the children's school and clubs this week, each with what he did and what he needs from you.
how: Open it from Ask → Daily. Ask Obi for an errand ("find someone to service the boiler") and he works on it step by step, searching the web; each errand's card says what he did, what he needs from you and any draft, with Copy and Stop. Each standing errand's button opens the screen that holds it; Turn off under Standing errands stops one.
tip: Obi never sends, books or buys anything from here: anything to send waits as a card for your tap.
tip: Ask or tell Talk "what are you working on?" for the same list out loud.
tip: Say "Obi, find someone to service the boiler" to the orb or type it in Ask: a Start card asks him to work on it, and Undo stops it.
tip: A draft is for you to send yourself: Copy puts it on the clipboard. Obi works on at most five errands at once, a few steps a day, and stops and asks before going on.
