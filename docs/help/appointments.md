what: the appointments you keep up with, with a nudge when one is due.
how: Tap + Add → Something you keep up with and type it once with how often and when you last went, e.g. "Dentist every 6 months, last 12 Apr"; A common one adds the dentist, a haircut or the optician in one tap.
tip: The three numbers up top are how many are due to book, how soon your next booked visit is and how many you keep up with.
tip: Tap an appointment to mark Went, type the day you've booked it for, or say when you last went; Remove is there too.
tip: Navilife books nothing and calls no one; when one comes due Needs you asks once.
