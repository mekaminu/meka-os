what: tasks you planned for an earlier day and didn't finish, at the top of Today so they never drop out of sight, each saying how long it has carried.
how: Tick one off, tap it to move it to another day, or leave it; moving it to a day ends the carry.
tip: After three days it asks once: Break it down (opens its steps), Someday, Drop (both with Undo) or Keep (it stops asking).
tip: Tasks with no day stay in Anytime today; done, dropped, Someday and snoozed ones never carry.
