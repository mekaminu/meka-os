what: the children's days off, PE and non-uniform days, trips and payments, with "who's covering?" a week ahead on office days.
how: Tap + Add, pick A day off, A school date or Every week and type one line ("INSET 4 Jan", "Alex PE Tue"), or The school's INSET days to paste them; the council's holidays are under Holidays from the council.
tip: The three numbers up top are how soon the next day off is, the days off to cover (or the school dates ahead) and the weekly things.
tip: Tap a row to see it whole, answer who's covering, put getting ready on a day with To a day…, or Remove it.
tip: The council's dates say "check with the school"; days you type always win.
tip: Add the school's website in About you and School links to it.
tip: The school's emails are known with no setup (its own address, its messaging service, or your children's school named in it): they stay in your inbox, never tidied, and their trips, payments, forms and days off show in Needs you with Add.
tip: Each new email from the school is a notification straight away, and waits in From the school, the first box in Needs you, until you've dealt with it. Add any other address it writes from under School's email in About you.
tip: A request with no date of its own, like "please send in a named coat", shows there too; Add makes it a task for the next school morning, not a School date.
tip: When the school writes that PE (or swimming, forest school, library books) is on certain days each week, each day shows there too; Add puts it in School as a weekly day, so Kids shows "PE kit" the day before.
tip: When the school writes that a date is cancelled or moved, what Navilife made for it (the School line, the tasks, the calendar entry) is taken out or moved to the new day, and Done for you says so, with Undo.
