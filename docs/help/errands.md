what: things to get or do when you're at a place.
how: Tap + Add → Things to do at a place and type the place, a colon, then what to do there, e.g. "Tesco: milk, batteries": the place shows under Waiting with how many to do.
tip: Tap a place to see its errands: Done ticks one off (it moves to Done lately, where Back puts it back) and Remove takes it off. The three numbers up top are errands waiting, the places they wait at and what you ticked off this week.
tip: On the Fold, tap Near-me reminders to turn them on, then open a place and tap I'm here while you're there: the phone reminds you as you arrive. Where you are stays on the phone.
