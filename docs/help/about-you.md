what: your name, partner, children (with their club age group), home, work, your train route, the school's website, the children's clubs and numbers, kept in your encrypted data, never in Navilife's code.
how: Type each line there; clearing one has Undo. Clubs takes each club's short name as your calendar writes it, then its full name ("OFC Oakfield Town, HRFC"): those fixtures get kit reminders and the child's name. Your partner's name is who Leaving now drafts to (unless you type another in Work mode) and whom Family makes the shopping list link for.
tip: Train route is the line from home, where you change and the next line, then after a semicolon the line you take instead ("South Rail to Oakfield Junction, then Metro line; or North Rail to Riverside"): Today, the brief, Leaving now and the train heads-ups follow those lines.
tip: Home and work are where the weather is for (unless you type another place in Calendars → Weather); the call assistant's number is the one Work mode and Setup show.
tip: Official numbers are your police force's and bank's switchboards, each with a word for whose it is ("police 01632 960000, bank 0306 999 0000"): a call from a number that looks like one gets "Could be spoofed" on its warning. They stay in your data.
