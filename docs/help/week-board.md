what: your tasks laid out a column per day, from this week's Monday for five weeks, with each day's events as faded chips above them and an Office, Home or Away marker under the date.
how: Open Calendar and tap Board at the top. Long-press a card and drag it to another day, or up and down in its day; let go and it lands there, with Undo on the bar. Tap a card for Move up, Move down and the next days instead.
mac_how: Click Board at the top of Calendar. Drag a card onto another card to take its place, or onto a day's free space to go last. Click a card to select it, then ⌘← ⌘→ move it a day and ⌘↑ ⌘↓ a place; right-click for the same moves.
tip: A card keeps its time when it moves to another day, and nothing can be moved into the past; Today's column holds tasks with no day, and ones carried over say so in gold.
tip: Your order is kept for each day and synced between the Fold and the Mac. "+ Add" at the foot of a day adds a task there; the ring ticks one off, and done ones stay faded at the foot of the day you did them.
tip: Tap a card (right-click on the Mac) and pick How long? — 15m, 30m, 1h or 2h; Navilife marks a guess from similar tasks as suggested, but nothing is set until you pick. Each day from today then shows "3h planned · 4h free" (free time is 09:00 to 21:00 less events, work and the commute) over a slim bar that turns amber when the day is over-planned.
tip: Above the days, "No day yet" holds tasks you want to do this week but not on a set day: drag a card there (or pick No day this week from its menu) and it leaves Today until you drag it onto a day; ones left from an earlier week wait in this week's lane saying so.
tip: The Someday tray beside it folds away your Someday tasks: drag a card onto it to keep it for later, or tap it open and drag one out onto a day.
tip: Today follows the order you set on the Board's Today column: the tasks with no time you placed come first in your order (the top one is Up next when nothing is planned at a time), then the rest as before.
tip: To block time for a task in your calendar, tap its card (right-click on the Mac) and pick a start under Block time: the free times that day for its estimate (30 minutes if it has none). The task is planned then and its block is added to your Google or Outlook calendar as busy time, only when you tap, with Undo on the bar; a task that already has a block moves it instead.
tip: To block time by dragging (Fold unfolded, or the Mac), drop a card on one of a day's free times under its events ("10:00–12:00 · 2h free"): it is blocked where you let go, on the quarter hour, moved to that day if it was elsewhere, and its block goes to your Google or Outlook calendar as busy time with Undo on the bar. A free time too short for the task says so and nothing moves.
tip: Across the top of the Board are the week's goals, up to three: "+ Goal" picks one of your open goals for that week or makes a new one; this week with none picked shows the same goals as Today's "This week" line. Tap a goal (right-click on the Mac) for Off this week.
tip: Drop a card on a goal (or pick it under For a goal in the card's menu) and the card says "◎ Run 5k"; each goal shows how many of that week's tasks for it are done, like "2 of 4 done". Every change has Undo.
tip: When three or more things land on one day (tasks due, bills and renewals, tasks carried over, evening events), that day's column wears an amber edge and says "Stacking up: Trip form, Council tax and Gym". One tap under it moves one of its tasks to a lighter day or does one of its due tasks today, with Undo.
tip: Within two days, the same "Friday is stacking up" line shows on Today and in the morning brief, with the same taps, and the orb mentions it when you say hello.
tip: To put an email on your week, open ⋯ More on it in From your mail and tap To a day…, then a day (Today, Tomorrow, the rest of this week, or next week's): it becomes a task last on that day's column, named from the subject, and the email is archived at the next check, with Undo on the bar.
tip: To put a Discord post on your week, tap To a day… under it in Needs you (beside Keep as idea), then a day: it becomes a task to look at it, last on that day's column, and the post reads "On your week". A note only, nothing is traded, with Undo on the bar.
tip: To give an overdue or due-today task a later day, tap To a day… under its card in Needs you, then a day: it leaves Needs you and sits last on that day's Board column, its due date kept (the line says "still due …"), with Undo on the bar.
