what: your tasks laid out a column per day, a week at a time for five weeks: each day has its big date and weekday (today underlined in gold), "2 of 5 done" over a thin gold bar, its events as slim chips ("09:30 · Dentist"), its free time as one quiet line, and its tasks as planner cards: the time and how long ("0:30") on top, the title, and the tick at the foot with its area ("● Family").
how: Open Calendar and tap Board at the top. Hold a card still for a moment until it lifts, then drag it to another day, or up and down in its day: the other cards slide apart with a gold line where it will land, and the board scrolls along near an edge; let go and it lands there, with Undo on the bar. Tap a card for Move up, Move down and the next days instead.
mac_how: Click Board at the top of Calendar. Drag a card onto another card to take its place, or onto a day's free space to go last. Click a card to select it, then ⌘← ⌘→ move it a day and ⌘↑ ⌘↓ a place; right-click for the same moves.
tip: A card keeps its time when it moves to another day, and nothing can be moved into the past; Today's column holds tasks with no day, and ones carried over say so in gold.
tip: Your order is kept for each day and synced between the Fold and the Mac. "+ Add a task" at the top of every day opens a field with the keyboard up: type, press Return and it is added last in that day, with the field still open for the next one; tap 15m, 30m, 1h or 2h under it first to give it an estimate, and tap anywhere else to close it.
tip: Tap the circle at the foot of a card to tick it off: it fills gold, the card fades (the words stay plain, nothing is struck through), and a moment later it settles below the open ones in the same day (one ticked off early stays on the day it was planned for). Tap it again to un-tick it, with Undo on the bar.
tip: Today ticks the same way: tick a task and it stays put, struck through, for a moment, then settles into "3 done today" at the foot of Today, still ticked. Tap its circle to un-tick it (Undo ticks it again).
tip: Tap a card (right-click on the Mac) and pick How long? — 15m, 30m, 1h or 2h; Navilife marks a guess from similar tasks as suggested, but nothing is set until you pick. Each day from today then shows a pill under its date, "planned · 4h 05 / 3h" (against its free time: 09:00 to 21:00 less events, work and the commute), its thin bar amber when the day is over-planned.
tip: On the unfolded Fold and the Mac, Monday to Friday sit across with Saturday and Sunday as two shorter columns underneath, then the No day yet and Someday lists; the cover screen shows one day at a time.
tip: Each card wears a coloured dot for the part of your life it belongs to: Family (orange), Work (blue), Side hustle (brass), Money (green) or House (violet). Navilife guesses from the title ("Boiler service" is House, "Send the quote" Side hustle); tap a card (right-click on the Mac) and pick one under Area, or No area, to set it, with Undo.
tip: Under the weekend, Someday splits into three lists: Someday, Side hustle and House. Drag a card onto Side hustle or House to keep it there for later (it leaves its day), or onto Someday to take it off those two; each move has Undo.
tip: Under the week's title Obi says one line about the week shown: "Mon is 1h over · move “Gym” to Fri?" when a day has more planned than free time (amber), "Thu is stacking up · …" when too much lands on one day, "3 tasks still need a day", or "Everything fits this week". When the line offers a move, tap it (click on the Mac) and that card goes there, with Undo on the bar.
tip: On the unfolded Fold and the Mac, ‹ › step a week (the week slides across); while you hold a card, the week scrolls down near the bottom edge so you can drop it on the weekend or a list (to move a card to another week, tap it, or right-click on the Mac, and pick a day).
tip: On the cover screen, above the day, the small chip "No day yet · 3" holds tasks you want to do this week but not on a set day: drag a card onto it (or pick No day this week from its menu) and it leaves Today until you drag it onto a day; ones left from an earlier week wait there saying so.
tip: On the cover screen, tap the No day yet chip to open its cards in a sheet from the bottom and drag one out onto a day's pill; tap it again to close it. On the unfolded Fold and the Mac, No day yet and Someday are always-open lists under the weekend: drag a card onto either, or out of one onto a day.
tip: On the cover screen the Someday chip beside it ("Someday · 12") keeps your Someday tasks folded away the same way: drag a card onto it to keep it for later, or tap it open and drag one out onto a day.
tip: On the unfolded Fold and the Mac, days already gone fold into narrow strips with their date and how many were done ("3 done"), so the rest of the week gets the room; tap one to open it, and tap its date to fold it again.
tip: Today follows the order you set on the Board's Today column: the tasks with no time you placed come first in your order (the top one is Up next when nothing is planned at a time), then the rest as before.
tip: To block time for a task in your calendar, tap its card (right-click on the Mac) and pick a start under Block time: the free times that day for its estimate (30 minutes if it has none). The task is planned then and its block is added to your Google or Outlook calendar as busy time, only when you tap, with Undo on the bar; a task that already has a block moves it instead.
tip: To block time by dragging (Fold unfolded, or the Mac), hold a card and drop it on one of a day's free times ("10:00–12:00 · 2h free"): it is blocked where you let go, on the quarter hour, moved to that day if it was elsewhere, and its block goes to your Google or Outlook calendar as busy time with Undo on the bar. A free time too short for the task says so and nothing moves.
tip: Across the top of the Board are the week's goals as small gold pills ("◎ Run 5k  2/4", filling gold as its tasks are done), up to three: "+ Goal" picks one of your open goals for that week or makes a new one; this week with none picked shows the same goals as Today's "This week" line. Tap a goal (right-click on the Mac) for Off this week.
tip: Drop a card on a goal (or pick it under For a goal in the card's menu) and the card says "◎ Run 5k"; each goal shows how many of that week's tasks for it are done, like "2 of 4 done". Every change has Undo.
tip: When three or more things land on one day (tasks due, bills and renewals, tasks carried over, evening events), that day's column wears an amber edge and says "Stacking up: Trip form, Council tax and Gym". One tap under it moves one of its tasks to a lighter day or does one of its due tasks today, with Undo.
tip: Within two days, the same "Friday is stacking up" line shows on Today and in the morning brief, with the same taps, and the orb mentions it when you say hello.
tip: To put an email on your week, open ⋯ More on it in From your mail and tap To a day…, then a day (Today, Tomorrow, the rest of this week, or next week's): it becomes a task last on that day's column, named from the subject, and the email is archived at the next check, with Undo on the bar.
tip: To put a Discord post on your week, tap To a day… under it in Needs you (beside Keep as idea), then a day: it becomes a task to look at it, last on that day's column, and the post reads "On your week". A note only, nothing is traded, with Undo on the bar.
tip: To give an overdue or due-today task a later day, tap To a day… under its card in Needs you, then a day: it leaves Needs you and sits last on that day's Board column, its due date kept (the line says "still due …"), with Undo on the bar.
tip: To get ready for a school date (a trip, a non-uniform day) or a birthday, tap To a day… under it in School, or in the opened birthday in Gifts, then a day up to the date itself: it becomes a task on that day's column ("Ready for Trip to the zoo · Fri 20 Nov" or "Gift for … · Sat 31 Oct", with the order-by day and your ideas in its notes), once per date, with Undo. Nothing is ordered.
tip: To plan your week, tap Plan your week above the Board (⇧⌘P on the Mac): what's left from last week, what's fixed each day, the week's goals, then a suggested spread that keeps office evenings light and never goes past a due day. Change any day or pick Leave; nothing moves until Accept plan, with one Undo. On Sunday it plans next week.
tip: On Sunday evening (at your week-ahead time, until Monday noon) Needs you shows Plan next week when something is left to place: Plan now opens the same flow, Later sets it aside, and once a plan is accepted on any device it goes. Saying "plan my week" to Obi opens it too; typed in Ask you get the suggested spread and where to accept it.
tip: From Friday, Review the week above this week's Board opens Review with the reflection open: under the week's facts it says how much of what you planned on the Board got done ("5 of 8 planned done · best day Tue, 3 done") and what kept slipping (tasks carried two days or more, or parked since last week), which also lead the What to drop suggestions.
tip: What you write as the one thing that matters next week is carried into Plan your week: its first step starts "From your review · Run 5k".
