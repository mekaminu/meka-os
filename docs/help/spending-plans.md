what: plans for things you want to buy or send (Christmas gifts, a holiday, money sent home), added up, with whether they fit your month.
how: Tap + New plan and type its name and the day it's needed by, like "Christmas gifts 20 Dec", then + Add each person or thing with how much and the month you'll buy it. Tick one when it's bought or sent. Can I afford it? takes what's still to buy from what Money says is left over each month, this month or spread over two or three.
tip: For money sent abroad, pick its currency when you make the plan, set Same for everyone (− / +) and type today's rate with Change rate.
tip: It's a calculator only: Navilife never sends or moves money.
