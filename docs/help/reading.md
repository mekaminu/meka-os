what: the books, podcasts and courses you want to get to, with how far you are through each one you've started.
how: Tap + Add → A book, podcast or course and type one line, e.g. "Atomic Habits by James Clear, 320 pages", "podcast The Rest Is History" or "course Kotlin coroutines 12 lessons": it goes under Up next. Tap it, then Start, and it's On the go.
tip: Tap one on the go and type how far you've got ("120", "p 120 of 320", "45%", "episode 4", "lesson 3"). The three numbers up top are what's on the go, what's up next and your goal for the year.
tip: Typing the last page (or 100%) finishes it; in its sheet Finished and Put down move it off the go, and Remove takes it off. Each has Undo.
tip: Start a line with "reading" ("reading Dune by Frank Herbert") and it goes straight on the go.
tip: Once anything moves in a month, the top of Reading says what you finished and what's next up.
tip: Ask or tell Talk "what am I reading?" for the list, or just say how far you've got: "I'm on page 200", "I'm halfway through Dune", "I've finished Atomic Habits", "add Dune to my reading list". It's done at once with Undo, without the AI (Ask first in What Navilife may do makes it a card).
tip: Share a book, podcast or course from another app (Goodreads, Audible, a bookshop, a podcast app, Udemy…) to Navilife: the sheet offers "📖 Add to Reading · …" and puts it under UP NEXT. On the Mac, select a title or link and choose Services → Add to Navilife Reading.
tip: Tap Reading goal (or + Add → A reading goal) and type how many books you'd like to finish this year ("12" or "24 books"): Reading and Goals show how many you've finished and whether you're on pace. Type "none" (or Clear the goal) to drop it.
tip: On an office day's commute the book (or podcast or course) on the go joins Today's train line with how far you are: "… · Dune at 37%".
