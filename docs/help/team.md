what: Navilife's helpers as a team: three tiles for how many are working, how many things need you and how much was done today; one Waiting on you box with only what needs you; and one Helpers box with a line per helper (Obi, Inbox, Calls, Money and Kestrel) saying what it did today.
how: Open it from Ask → Daily. Tap a Waiting on you row to open it (a trade goes to Needs you). Tap a helper's line to unfold its role, its last three things and Open · Pause · Whole day ›; Whole day shows today's full log.
tip: Nothing on this screen acts for you. Kestrel only reads here: a trade is answered in Needs you, with your fingerprint, and Navilife never approves one by itself.
tip: Pause on Obi's, Inbox's or Money's line (or Pause for today on its Whole day page) makes it do less till midnight: its heads-ups wait for your next digest, a paused Obi doesn't speak up and a paused Inbox tidies nothing. Reminders you set still come; Turn back on (or Resume) starts it again now.
tip: Ask the team… at the foot talks to Obi: a tap puts the cursor in Ask's field (the mic starts him listening), and for a few minutes what you ask carries the whole team, so "anything need me?" gets each helper's day. Obi also sees the team whenever you ask about it, on the orb too.
