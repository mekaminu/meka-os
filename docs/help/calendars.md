what: the connected calendars and feeds, and the places the weather is for.
how: Connect Google Calendar there; fixtures and other feeds are listed with when they last synced.
tip: Each Barça match in the Fixtures calendar says its kick-off in UK time and where to watch it in the UK ("On UK TV: Premier Sports 1", "Not on UK TV" or "not listed yet") in its notes, and an hour before kick-off a heads-up says the same. Lower "Barça match day" in Ask → Settings → Notifications; hide a match (or the Fixtures calendar) and it says nothing.
tip: While a Barça match is on, its notes in the Calendar lead with the live score and who scored ("Live: Barça 2–1 Getafe · 67'", then "Full time: …"), News leads with it, and each goal and the final score come as a heads-up in one place that updates. For goals only or full time only, lower "Barça full time" or "Barça goals" in Ask → Settings → Notifications.
tip: The morning brief says last night's Barça result and who scored for Barça ("Last night Barça beat Getafe two–one, with goals from Lewandowski and Yamal"), and on a match day which channel it's on. Ask or Talk "what's the score?", "did Barça win?" or "what channel is Barça on?" for the match in your calendar; without the internet Navilife answers from the match's notes itself.
tip: Editing an event that repeats (a weekly gym session, say) asks **This event · Every event** above When: Every event renames it, changes its place, notes or time of day (each keeps its own day) and Delete every event removes the whole series, in Google, after the five-second Undo. Moving it to another day is for This event only.
tip: **This and following** (on a timed Google series) changes or deletes this event and every one after it, leaving the earlier ones as they were: Google ends the series the day before and starts a new one from this day.
tip: An event on a calendar shared with you to view only (a holidays calendar, or someone else's) shows **Edit** greyed with why under it: its owner changes it in Google or Outlook. If Edit is greyed because editing is off for that account, Allow editing in Ask → Settings → Calendars.
tip: Ask or Talk "move the dentist to 4pm" (or "push my haircut to Friday at 10") and Navilife offers a **Move** card for that event; nothing changes until you tap Move, then it moves in Google or Outlook after the five-second Undo, keeping its length. It offers this for your own events today and in the next fortnight that have no guests; a meeting with guests is moved from its Edit.
tip: If Google or Outlook signs you out while a change is still to send, it waits instead of failing: the event shows "Waiting to move … · reconnect Google in Calendars and it goes then", and once you Reconnect in Ask → Settings → Calendars every waiting change goes within a minute, in the order you made them. After a week of waiting it gives up and says so.
tip: An event set in another time zone (a call with New York, or a home event while you're abroad and the phone has moved to local time) shows a second line on its detail with its time there: "New York time · 09:00–10:00", naming the day when it's a different day there. Events whose clock matches yours, and all-day events, show nothing extra.
tip: On the Mac, drag one of your own events onto a day in the week strip (or a day's heading) and it moves there, keeping its time; in an event's detail ⌘E edits it, ⌥⌘← and ⌥⌘→ move it a day, ⌥⌘↑ and ⌥⌘↓ fifteen minutes, each with Undo. Meetings with guests move from their Edit. ⌥⌘N adds an event.
