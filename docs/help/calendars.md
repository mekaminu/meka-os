what: the connected calendars and feeds, and the places the weather is for.
how: Connect Google Calendar there; fixtures and other feeds are listed with when they last synced.
tip: Each Barça match in the Fixtures calendar says its kick-off in UK time and where to watch it in the UK ("On UK TV: Premier Sports 1", "Not on UK TV" or "not listed yet") in its notes, and an hour before kick-off a heads-up says the same. Lower "Barça match day" in Ask → Settings → Notifications; hide a match (or the Fixtures calendar) and it says nothing.
tip: While a Barça match is on, its notes in the Calendar lead with the live score and who scored ("Live: Barça 2–1 Getafe · 67'", then "Full time: …"), News leads with it, and each goal and the final score come as a heads-up in one place that updates. For goals only or full time only, lower "Barça full time" or "Barça goals" in Ask → Settings → Notifications.
