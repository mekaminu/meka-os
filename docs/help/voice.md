what: how Obi, Navilife's assistant, sounds in Talk, the morning brief and the call assistant.
how: Pick a voice there and play a sample.
tip: On a slow connection the first words can take a moment; offline it says "Using the phone's voice while offline" once and carries on in the phone's own voice.
tip: Obi talks a touch quicker than before, with a small natural pause after a quick reaction ("Oh nice."), and each sentence of his answer is made ready as he writes it, so there are no gaps between them (Amazon voices; Google's voices keep their own pace).
tip: Ask → More → Activity shows how quickly the voice answered; clips heard over Bluetooth or car audio are marked "+ Bluetooth", about 0.2 s more.
tip: In Talk the first words of an answer come with the answer itself, so Navilife starts speaking without a second wait; Activity marks those "with the answer".
tip: At the top, Obi calls you lets you choose whether it calls you by your name or "sir" in Ask, Talk and Hello; the choice follows you to every device.
tip: Under it, What Obi remembers lists a line about each proper chat (kept 30 days in your own data) so Ask, Talk and Hello can pick up where you left off; choose Don't remember to start fresh each time (it forgets them too), or Forget all.
tip: At the foot of Obi's voice, Speed shows your last 20 hellos and spoken answers: how long each took to start (aim: a hello within 0.6 s of your tap, an answer within 1.5 s of you finishing) and where the wait went (sending, the answer, the voice). It is kept on this device only and holds times, never what was said; Clear empties it.
tip: Under Speed, On the server shows how long Navilife's server took over the last day's answers, quick look-ups and conversation apart: the middle time to the first words, the first sentence (aim: within 1 second), the voice ready and the whole answer. Times only, never what was said.
tip: Spoken answers, and every quick look-up, come from Obi's quicker model so his first sentence starts sooner; a typed open question (advice, planning, a long one) still gets the larger, more thoughtful one. His long standing instructions and the conversation so far are reused from one turn to the next rather than read afresh, which is quicker and costs less.
tip: A tap on the orb speaks at once: the hello is made ahead in Obi's voice and kept ready on this device (for this part of the day and the next hour), and while the first sentence of an answer plays the rest is already on its way, so there is no pause between them.
tip: While Obi works out an answer he never says "One sec" or any other holding line: the orb shows Thinking at once, and only if an answer takes more than about two and a half seconds does one soft, breath-like tone play. Most answers start well before that, so there is just the orb and then his voice.
tip: The moment you tap the orb, open Talk or start speaking, Navilife's server gets its line to the AI and to Obi's voice ready, so the first answer doesn't wait for a fresh connection. Nothing you say is sent for this, and it costs nothing.
tip: On the phone, a quick question that only looks something up ("what's on tomorrow?", "when's the bin day?") goes off the moment Navilife hears you finish, while the phone is still writing down your last words; if those words turn out different, it asks again with them. Anything that adds, moves, sends or pays, chats and Money questions always wait for your final words.
tip: With headphones (AirPods, a Bluetooth headset, the car) you can answer the hello while it is still speaking: the microphone opens with it, ignores Navilife's own voice and stops the hello once you say a couple of words, so none of yours are lost. On the speaker, talking over the hello stops it a little quicker than other lines.
tip: Obi's voice → Live (off until you turn it on) lets you talk with Obi in real time on Google's Gemini Live: no waiting for his turn, and you can talk over him any time. Starting Talk opens a Live conversation; tap the orb to end it. Pick his Live voice under the switch.
tip: If Live can't open within two seconds (no signal, the month's £15 used up, its key not set up yet), Obi answers as he normally does. While Live is on your voice and today's Navilife context go to Google; Navilife keeps no recordings, only how many seconds were heard and spoken.
tip: Under Obi's Live voice each voice has ▶ Sample: it plays the start of today's brief in Google's studio voice of the same name, which Live's voice is built on. If Google's voices can't be reached it says so rather than playing a different voice.
tip: In Live, Obi starts each talk knowing your day and your name, and looks up anything current (news, scores, travel) with the web search Ask uses; a search about your own life or family is never sent. Each answer shows in Ask with what he said and where it came from.
tip: In Live, Obi can look at another day himself ("what's on Thursday?") from your calendar and tasks on the phone, and offer a change ("add a task", "move the dentist to four") as Ask's usual card: nothing happens until you tap it, and he can never send, pay, book or trade.
tip: With Live on, "Hello, Navilife" (the orb, the phrase, or the bedside clock's orb) still greets you at once, then Obi carries on live: just answer him. A long Live talk keeps going past ten minutes while you're talking (up to about an hour); after two quiet minutes it ends at the next break.
tip: In Live, Obi can check your email ("anything from the school?") from the mail on your phone, and answer money questions ("what did I spend on takeaways?") from Money while it is unlocked; locked, he says so and Ask shows Unlock. Emails are other people's words, so he never acts on one by himself.
tip: On the Mac, Live is the same switch and voices under Obi's voice, and clicking Talk's orb opens a Live talk (click it again to end). The Mac's echo canceller keeps his own voice out of the microphone on the speakers. With Live on, the Mac's hello (clicking the orb at rest, or saying "Hello Obi") still greets you at once, then Obi carries on live: just answer him, or click the orb to end.
