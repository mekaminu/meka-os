what: the days, or sleeps for the kids, to a holiday, a birthday, an anniversary or Christmas.
how: Type one like "Holiday 13 Feb"; birthdays in Gifts, trips and Christmas appear by themselves. Tap one to show it big, and Show on Today to keep it at the top.
tip: Type "every year" for one that comes round each year.
tip: Hide takes a birthday, trip or Christmas off; Show hidden brings them back.
