what: connects Gmail and Outlook: newsletters, receipts and promos are tidied away, what needs you comes to Needs you, and bills and parcels are picked up.
how: Tap Connect and sign in with Google or Microsoft. Each tidy has Undo, and Autopilot can be paused per account.
tip: Nothing is sent or deleted without your tap.
tip: Parcels from shipping emails show on Today on their day (with "you're at the office" on an office day), and one due yesterday that no email called delivered shows as "Not arrived?" for a day; ask "any parcels coming?" or "where's my Amazon parcel?" and the morning brief says them too.
tip: Disconnect can also delete that account's mail data.
tip: In Needs you, Draft replies drafts a reply to each email for you to read; Send all then sends the short ones, each from its own account, with Undo.
tip: A renewal email (a subscription or policy about to renew) shows in Needs you under Renewals from your mail; Add puts it on Lists → Renewals with its day and price, Not needed clears it.
tip: An order email that says how long you have to send it back puts "Last day to return" on Lists → Renewals, with a heads-up two days before and on the day; tap Sent it back and the refund goes on Waiting for, or Keeping it to take it off the radar.
tip: When the shop emails that your refund has been made, Needs you shows Refund from the shop with what it closes on Waiting for; Close it marks the refund received (Undo puts it back), Not this one leaves it waiting.
tip: A promise in an email (“I'll send it by Friday”) shows there too; Add puts it on Waiting for, chased the day after the day they gave.
tip: The life audit reads the last 60 days of your mail for payments that repeat: each subscription shows in Needs you with its price, how often you paid and the next payment, and the card adds up what they cost a month; Add puts one on Lists → Renewals, Not needed clears it for good.
tip: Mail → Life audit → Review shows what 60 days of your mail turned up as one review: what was read, your subscriptions and what they cost a month, emails waiting on you and who needs a reply, what others owe you, warranties and bills due in the next 30 days, each saying where it lives. It only counts: nothing is paid, cancelled or sent.
tip: An email you sent asking something that's had no reply for 3 days shows there as No reply yet; Add puts it on Waiting for to chase, and it goes by itself once they answer.
tip: Some people take longer to answer: tap Wait 3 days on a No reply yet row and pick 1 day to 2 weeks for that person; their emails wait that long before Navilife offers a chase, on both apps.
tip: To see or change everyone you've set a wait for, open Mail settings: Chase waits lists each person with the same chips and Back to usual (3 days), with Undo.
tip: Once it's on Waiting for, Time to chase offers a short follow-up to send in the same conversation, to the people you wrote to, from the same account; nothing goes until you tap Send, and Undo stops it.
tip: With Navilife's AI on, tapping Chase writes the follow-up from your email (who, the subject, how long it's been) in your usual tone; change anything, tap Another draft for a different one, and still nothing goes until you tap Send.
tip: An email asking to meet says "Asks to meet" in Needs you; Suggest times opens the reply with three times you are free from your calendars, sent only when you tap Send.
tip: If they can't make the times you offered, their answer says so with Suggest times for other days; if their email says where they are ("I'm in New York"), the times are also written in their time and kept inside their working day.
tip: When they write back picking one of the times you offered, their email says "Sam picked Tue 20 Oct, 13:00–14:00"; Book it puts it in your calendar (Undo for five seconds), and nothing is sent to them.
tip: To have renewals and promises from your mail added for you, turn on Renewals and promises from mail in What Navilife may do; each one shows in Activity with Undo, and an undone one comes back to Needs you with Add.
tip: Fifteen minutes before a meeting with other people, Today shows Prep: who is coming, the last email from each of them and what you promised them or are waiting on from them; a heads-up says the same.
tip: Prep also says when you last met each of them ("Last met Tue 6 Oct"); choose how early it comes (5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes) or turn it off under Meeting prep in Mail.
tip: Prep finds each guest's last email in your connected mailboxes themselves (the last 60 days), not only the emails Navilife has already shown you.
tip: On an "Empty Junk" card in Needs you, Review lists who the emails going to Trash are from; take the tick off a sender to keep their mail in Junk, then Approve there.
tip: On an email in Needs you, Block sender sends it and the sender's later emails straight to Trash (recoverable for 30 days), with Undo; Navilife never blocks people you write to, your partner, banks, schools or public bodies. On a possible scam the button reads Bin & block.
tip: An email shows Reply, Done and Task; tap ⋯ More for Waiting for, Snooze, Unsubscribe, Always tidy and Block sender. A link you email yourself shows as a Note to self with Save as task, which keeps the link in the task's notes.
tip: Always tidy on an email archives it, and that sender's future emails skip your inbox and go to Archive. Unsubscribe (when the email offers it) unsubscribes you the sender's own way. Change any of these in Mail → Your rules.
tip: Tap an email on From your mail or in Archived for you to read the whole of it inside Navilife: who it's from with the address, To and Cc, the full email and its attachments. Pictures from the web stay hidden until you tap Show images, and a link only opens after you see where it really goes. Reading it doesn't mark it read.
tip: In the email reader, Open in Gmail or Open in Outlook takes you to the same email in its own app. Reading an email in Navilife leaves it unread in your mail unless you turn on Mark an email read when I open it in Mail.
tip: Tap an attachment in the email reader to open it: a photo or PDF opens inside Navilife, a Word file or spreadsheet goes to another app after you say yes, and anything that could run a program is never opened from Navilife.
