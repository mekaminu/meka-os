what: the facts Navilife answers from: car registration, GP, sizes and the like.
how: Tap + Add, pick A fact (or A fact kept from the AI), and type one line: "Car reg: AB12 CDE".
tip: The three numbers up top are how many facts you've kept, how many Obi can use and how many are kept from the AI.
tip: Facts sit in groups — Sizes, Health and care, Car, Home and utilities, Family and school, Other — five to a card with See all for the rest.
tip: Tap a fact to see it whole, type a new one to change it, Copy it for a form, keep it from the AI or Remove it.
tip: Passwords, PINs and card, bank or ID numbers are turned away; write where they're kept instead.
