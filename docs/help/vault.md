what: your documents and the day each runs out, reminded when the renewal window opens.
how: Type the document and its expiry, e.g. "passport 20 May 2027"; a passport is reminded six months ahead, then a month, a week and on the day.
tip: Document numbers aren't kept, only the name and the date.
tip: Or share a photo or screenshot of the document to Navilife (or photograph it with Capture → 📷 Photo of a letter): it reads the name and expiry on the device and offers 🗂 Add to the Vault.
tip: Tap a document → Add a copy to keep a photo or PDF of it (1.4 MB at most). It is locked with your Vault key on the device before it leaves, so Navilife's server only holds locked bytes; Open copy shows it in the app, even offline.
tip: A receipt Inbox autopilot tidied can be kept here too: open it in Archived for you (or All tidied mail) → Keep in the Vault. It shows what it cost, when you bought it and the warranty's end when the email says, with Open the email; Take it out of the Vault on the same email undoes it.
tip: Your documents are locked with one Vault key kept only on your phone and Mac. Under VAULT KEY a new phone or Mac asks for it with a six-digit code: Allow on a device that has the key only if both show the same code. A new recovery key opens it too.
tip: Ask or tell Talk "when does my passport run out?", "do I have a copy of my licence?" or "what's in my Vault?": the answer comes from the Vault on this device (never the AI), with Open the Vault under it, even offline.
