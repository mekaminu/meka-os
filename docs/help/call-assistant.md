what: while you're at work, calls from people who aren't family, the always-ring list or a repeat caller go to a cloud voice assistant that says you're at work and takes a message.
how: Turn it on under Work mode → Call assistant, then set your network's forward-when-busy to the assistant's number (Setup lists it). The messages come to Needs you with a summary.
mac_how: The Mac shows the messages and the settings; the screening itself happens on the Fold.
tip: Family and the always-ring list always ring through, and a second call within 3 minutes rings.
tip: It only takes messages: it never shares your details or agrees to anything.
