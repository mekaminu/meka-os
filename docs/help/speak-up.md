what: for a few moments that really matter, Ada says it out loud on her own in a sentence or two, starting with your name: your train cancelled or 10 minutes or more late before you leave, and time to leave for an event or a booked session with its travel time set.
how: Each moment has its own switch there, all on to start. Ada only speaks through headphones, earbuds or the car, on the bedside clock in the evening, or on the Mac while you're using it.
mac_how: On the Mac she speaks only while you're using it (a key or the mouse touched in the last 2 minutes); the switches are the same as the phone's and follow you.
tip: Never out of the phone's own speaker, in quiet hours, in a meeting (an event with other people or a video link), on a call, or while you're talking to Ada; and never more than 6 a day (choose Up to 3 a day under How often).
tip: Mute for 2 hours keeps her quiet on every device; the notifications still arrive as usual.
tip: She only tells you: anything to do is the usual notification or card. Everything she says on her own is in Activity, marked Said aloud.
tip: A tap on the docked orb while she speaks stops her and says hello as usual.
