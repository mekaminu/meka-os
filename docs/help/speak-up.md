what: for a few moments that really matter, Ada says it out loud on her own in a sentence or two, starting with your name: an urgent message from family or Always notify, your train cancelled or 10 minutes or more late before you leave, time to leave for an event or booked session, once in the evening, that tomorrow is stacking up, and, if you turn it on, a reply you were waiting for.
how: Each moment has its own switch there, all on to start except A reply you were waiting for. Ada only speaks through headphones, earbuds or the car, on the bedside clock in the evening, or on the Mac while you're using it.
mac_how: On the Mac she speaks only while you're using it (a key or the mouse touched in the last 2 minutes); the switches are the same as the phone's and follow you. Urgent from family is said by the Fold, where work mode's alert arrives.
tip: Never out of the phone's own speaker, in quiet hours, in a meeting (an event with other people or a video link), on a call, or while you're talking to Ada; and never more than 6 a day (choose Up to 3 a day under How often).
tip: Mute for 2 hours keeps her quiet on every device; the notifications still arrive as usual. Saying "shh" or "not now" to Ada in Talk does the same.
tip: Urgent from family is said only for someone on your family or Always notify list whose message says it's urgent (strangers never), with the start of their words; it's the same alert work mode lets through.
tip: She only tells you: anything to do is the usual notification or card. Everything she says on her own is in Activity, marked Said aloud.
tip: A tap on the docked orb while she speaks stops her and says hello as usual. On the bedside clock its orb lights up as she speaks, and a tap there just stops her.
tip: Tomorrow stacking up comes at 19:30 the evening before a day with three or more things on it (tasks due, bills, carried tasks, evening events): "Meka, tomorrow is stacking up: Trip form, Council tax and Gym. Today has a way to lighten it." It is a notification too, and Today's stacking-up line has the one-tap fix.
tip: A reply you were waiting for is off to start. When someone answers an email of yours on Waiting for, a notification "Sam Taylor replied" comes either way; turned on, Ada also says "Meka, Sam Taylor replied to your email about Quote for the kitchen." within the hour. Only their name and your subject are said, never their words. Got it in Time to chase settles it.
