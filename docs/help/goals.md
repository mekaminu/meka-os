what: your habits with the week so far, your goals with their milestones, and fasting.
how: Add a habit or a goal there; Navilife can suggest milestones and first steps for a goal, and nothing is added until you tick the ones to keep.
tip: Start a fast there, or ask "start a 16-hour fast".
tip: A goal with a £ number can follow a Money account's balance: link it under MONEY GOALS in Money, and its number moves with the account each time you open Money.
tip: Money challenges you set in Money (no-spend days, fun money, a category cap) show under MONEY CHALLENGES as streaks with no amounts; tap one to open Money.
tip: A reading goal set in Reading ("12 books this year") shows under READING GOAL with how many you've finished and your pace; tap it to open Reading.
