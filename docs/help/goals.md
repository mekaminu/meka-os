what: your habits with the week so far, your goals with their milestones, and fasting.
how: Add a habit or a goal there; Navilife can suggest milestones and first steps for a goal, and nothing is added until you tick the ones to keep.
tip: Start a fast there, or ask "start a 16-hour fast".
tip: While a fast runs, Today shows its card: the ring with your time, the stage you're in and when it ends. Tap it for the Fasting screen, or End to finish (with Undo).
tip: Say or type "start my fast" (or "start a 16-hour fast") to start one, "how long left on my fast?" (or "am I fasting?", "when can I eat?") for how long you're in, the stage you're in and how long to go, and "end my fast" to end it; "resume my fast" within 10 minutes takes an ending back. These work without the AI, offline too.
tip: A goal with a £ number can follow a Money account's balance: link it under MONEY GOALS in Money, and its number moves with the account each time you open Money.
tip: Money challenges you set in Money (no-spend days, fun money, a category cap) show under MONEY CHALLENGES as streaks with no amounts; tap one to open Money.
tip: A reading goal set in Reading ("12 books this year") shows under READING GOAL with how many you've finished and your pace; tap it to open Reading.
tip: Under STUDY, add a course you're working through, open it and paste its outline: one lesson a line, a line like "Module 2: Core skills" starts a module, and "45 min" at the end says how long a lesson takes. Navilife splits it into modules and lessons; nothing leaves your data.
tip: In a course, YOUR PLAN asks when you want to finish, how long a session is and when you can study (train mornings, Thursday after work, Saturday morning, evenings, nights). It works out how many sessions a week that takes, whether it fits the times you picked (and what would make it fit), and shows under the course's name whether you're on track or how many lessons behind.
tip: Tick a lesson when you've done it: the ring and "5 of 24 lessons · about 18 h left" move at once, on the Fold and the Mac, and a module reads Done once all its lessons are ticked.
tip: Press and hold a lesson (right-click on the Mac) to rename it, move it to another module, mark it done or delete it; Delete always asks first, and Undo or Recently deleted under the course brings it back for 30 days.
tip: Press and hold a module's heading to rename or delete it. "+ Add lesson" at the foot of an open module adds into that module (one, or paste several lines); "+ Add module" adds a module with its first lessons.
tip: To plan a lesson for a day, press and hold it (right-click on the Mac) and pick Today, Tomorrow or a day this week under Plan for. It shows on Today ("Study today · 2 lessons · about 1 h · next: Pivot tables"), in Tomorrow, and on the course with a date chip; a lesson not done on its day moves on to today, marked "from Mon". Not planned takes it off.
