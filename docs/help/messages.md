what: Navilife reads your WhatsApp notifications (never WhatsApp itself) and groups them into chat cards in Needs you, with a reply you send yourself.
how: Give Navilife notification access when Work mode → Messages asks, and keep WhatsApp's notifications on (WhatsApp settings there shows how).
tip: Nothing is ever sent without your tap.
tip: If cards stop arriving, check Navilife still has notification access in Android's settings.
tip: Send goes through WhatsApp's own notification while it's still in the shade; once it's gone (or on the Mac), Reply in WhatsApp opens that person's chat with your reply typed and you tap send there. Each card says which way its reply will go.
tip: A message waiting for your reply keeps its notification in the shade until you send or finish its card, so Send keeps working.
tip: When WhatsApp gives only a name, the Fold finds that person's number on your phone (Family and always-notify first, then a contact of exactly that name), so Open chat lands in their chat with your reply typed, even hours later. Two people of that name: no guess, your reply is copied.
tip: Once a group's message has gone from the shade, WhatsApp can't be opened into that group: the button says Open WhatsApp and names the group to find.
tip: On the Mac, Send (via your phone) sends your reply through your phone while the message is still showing there; if it was opened on your phone first, the card says so and offers Reply in WhatsApp instead.
