what: a card for each child with their sizes (shoes, football boots, tops, trousers, coat, school jumper), a nudge to check them every three months, their club fees with when each is due, their chores and pocket money if you want them, and THIS WEEK: what they need in the next 7 days.
how: Add the children in Ask → Settings → About you first. Then open Kids, type a size under a child (e.g. "13" for shoes or "7-8 years" for tops) and press Done. Clear a size to remove it; Undo puts it back.
tip: Typing a size counts as checking that child's sizes. Three months later the Kids tile lights "Check Alex's sizes": type the new ones, or tap All still fit if nothing has changed.
tip: On the Saturday after the three months are up, at 9 in the morning, a heads-up says "Time to check Alex's sizes" with what you kept and when; tap All still fit on it if nothing has changed, or tap it to open Kids and type the new ones. Left unchecked, it asks again four weeks later.
tip: Sizes are kept in your own data (synced, encrypted) and show on the Fold and the Mac alike.
tip: Under CLUB FEES type one line such as "Football £35 monthly from 1 Nov" or "Swimming £60 on the 5th every month" and press Done. Each fee is a bill on the radar too, so you get one reminder three days before (Lists → Renewals shows it); Paid moves it to next time and Remove takes it off, both with Undo. Navilife never pays anything.
tip: THIS WEEK at the top of Kids lists the next 7 days from School (PE as "PE kit", trips, days off), the club sessions and matches in your calendar (named by the age group, or by whose series you set) and their fees due. Kit for tomorrow and fees are in brass.
tip: Ask or tell Navilife "what size shoes is Alex?", "what do the boys need this week?" or "when's the football fee due?": it answers from Kids (sizes, fees and THIS WEEK), and a size is answered on the device even when the AI can't be reached.
tip: Under CHORES AND POCKET MONEY type a weekly amount ("£5") and chores such as "Tidy room £1" or just "Feed the cat". Tap a day's dot to tick a chore (today, or an earlier day this week); the card adds up the week ("£5 pocket money + £3 for chores = £8"). Paid notes what you gave them, with Undo, and on Sunday the Kids tile reminds you of anything still to pay. Navilife never moves money.
