what: a card for each child with their sizes (shoes, football boots, tops, trousers, coat, school jumper), a nudge to check them every three months, and their club fees with when each is due.
how: Add the children in Ask → Settings → About you first. Then open Kids, type a size under a child (e.g. "13" for shoes or "7-8 years" for tops) and press Done. Clear a size to remove it; Undo puts it back.
tip: Typing a size counts as checking that child's sizes. Three months later the Kids tile lights "Check Alex's sizes": type the new ones, or tap All still fit if nothing has changed.
tip: Sizes are kept in your own data (synced, encrypted) and show on the Fold and the Mac alike.
tip: Under CLUB FEES type one line such as "Football £35 monthly from 1 Nov" or "Swimming £60 on the 5th every month" and press Done. Each fee is a bill on the radar too, so you get one reminder three days before (Lists → Renewals shows it); Paid moves it to next time and Remove takes it off, both with Undo. Navilife never pays anything.
