what: who to call, where important documents are and key accounts by name, ready when it matters.
how: Tap + Add, pick Someone to call, Where something is or A key account, and type one line: "GP: Dr Patel, 01632 123456".
tip: The three numbers up top are how many to call, things placed and key accounts.
tip: Tap a line to see it whole, type new details to change it, or Remove it.
tip: Passwords, PINs and card or account numbers are turned away; write where they're kept instead.
