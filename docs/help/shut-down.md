what: an evening moment: what got done, what carries over to tomorrow, tomorrow's first thing and the wake alarm.
how: It comes as a card at work's end (20:00 on other days); open it and tap Shut down.
tip: The wake alarm it suggests counts the commute and your time to get ready.
tip: On the Fold it can end with a photo from this date in past years (On this day): Turn on at the bottom of the pane. The photo stays on the phone.
