what: your week looked back on: what got done, habits, and the misses Navilife helped prevent, with a two-minute reflection kept as a dated journal.
how: Open it any time; the arrows step back through earlier weeks.
tip: Reflect (near the top) is a two-minute look back: what went well, what to drop, and one thing that matters next week. It opens by itself from Sunday 18:00 (or the time you choose) through Monday; any other time tap "Reflect on your week".
tip: The chips under each question come from your week (wins, things you ticked off, habits met, what's still open, your goals): tap one to add it. Type or use the keyboard's mic, then Save. One line in any of the three is enough.
tip: Saving keeps it in "Your reflections" (open it from an earlier week), adds what went well to Wins (untick "Also keep in Wins" to stop that) and, if you tapped a goal's chip for the one thing, makes it one of next week's goals. "Skip this week" puts it away until next Sunday.
tip: Say "reflect on my week" to Talk (on the phone or the Mac) and Review opens on this week (last week on a Monday) with Reflect open, any day. Nothing is kept until you tap Save.
tip: The one thing you said matters shows under Today's header all the next week ("This week's one thing · …", in brass on the Monday), and the morning brief says it on Monday (and every weekday on Standard and Full).
tip: To have the review and reflection come at another time on Sunday, tap Change beside "Comes on Sundays at 18:00" at the bottom of Reflect (on the Mac, pick the time there) and choose 16:00 to 21:00. The review's heads-up and Today's card move with it, on both apps.
tip: Not now on a Sunday? Tap "Later tonight" on Today's review card or on its notification: the card steps away and the review comes back two hours on (never after 22:00), on the phone and the Mac.
tip: Balance (under the numbers) shows the week's time across work, family, health and yourself against your targets, from your calendar, your work hours and the habits you ticked. Targets › sets each one (Your hours for work); they follow on both apps. Under each area four small bars show the last four weeks (brass when met) with how often you met it and your usual week.
tip: When family, health or your own time is well short of its target even with what's still planned, one heads-up comes on Thursday at 17:00, once a week at most; lower or silence it in Settings → Notifications under Balance.
