what: where you parked and how to walk back to it, a parking timer that reminds you 10 minutes before your paid time ends, and each car's MOT, tax, insurance and service dates.
how: When you leave the car, open Ask → Life → Car and tap "I've parked here" (with two cars, pick which). Type the bay from the sign, e.g. "level 3 row F". To get back, tap Find my car: an arrow and the distance point the way, and the phone buzzes once you're within 15 m. Drove off clears it. Or say "where did I park?" to Talk.
mac_how: The Mac's Car tile opens the parking timer (set, extend or end it; it's shared with the phone) and each car's dates, which open Lists → Renewals; where you parked is kept on the phone only.
tip: Where you park never leaves the phone: it isn't synced or sent. Walk there in Maps hands the spot to your maps app only when you tap it.
tip: Car's Bluetooth: in the Car pane under BLUETOOTH, Pick each car's Bluetooth (allow Nearby devices). When it disconnects after a drive, Navilife saves where you parked by itself if location is allowed “All the time”, otherwise a notification "Parked the Golf?" saves it with one tap. When it connects again later, the spot is cleared.
tip: Underground the phone may not know where it is: type the bay, or tap Photo of the bay on the parked card and take a picture of the bay or pillar sign: the phone reads the sign ("Level 3 · Row F") if you haven't typed the bay, and the photo shows over Find my car's arrow. It stays on the phone (never synced, sent or backed up), and goes when you drive off.
tip: With two cars, say the car's name to Talk ("where's the Golf?", "find car 2"): Find my car opens on that car, or the pane says it isn't saved as parked.
tip: Hold the phone flat for the arrow; if it spins, wave the phone in a figure of eight to settle its compass.
tip: Parking timer: type how long you paid for or until when ("2 h", "until 15:30"); a reminder comes 10 minutes before it ends. +15 min, +30 min or +1 h extend it, Stop timer clears it.
tip: Type the RingGo or JustPark location code beside the time ("until 6pm RingGo 81234") and the reminder shows it.
tip: Paid in RingGo, JustPark or PayByPhone? Share the confirmation (the app's or the email's text) to Navilife, or paste it into the timer field: Navilife reads when it ends on the phone and offers "Parking timer until 15:30 · RingGo 81234"; nothing starts until you tap it. With two cars, the registration in it picks the car, otherwise it asks which.
tip: FUEL NEARBY in the Car pane shows the cheapest forecourts within 8 km for the fuel each car takes (Change under the prices picks unleaded, super unleaded, diesel or premium diesel), from the government's Fuel Finder; tap one for directions. Only a point rounded to about 1 km goes to Navilife's server, and nothing is kept.
tip: On the Galaxy Watch a running parking timer shows under Capture ("Paid until 15:30 · 40 min left") with +15 min, on the tile and in the complication's ring; where you parked stays on the phone.
