what: trips and their bookings, day by day.
how: Type a trip with its dates ("Barcelona 14–18 Nov"), then each booking with its day, time and reference.
tip: Share a booking confirmation to Navilife and Handle this offers to add it to the right trip.
tip: Open a trip: TRAVEL DOCUMENTS lists the passports, ID and health cards in your Vault (a driving licence when a car is booked, and travel insurance) and says whether each lasts the trip; a passport with under six months left when you're back is lit. Open copy shows a kept copy after your fingerprint (Touch ID on the Mac), offline too. Ask "are our passports OK for Barcelona?" for the same answer.
