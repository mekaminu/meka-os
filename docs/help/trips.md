what: trips and their bookings, day by day.
how: Type a trip with its dates ("Barcelona 14–18 Nov"), then each booking with its day, time and reference.
tip: Share a booking confirmation to Navilife and Handle this offers to add it to the right trip.
tip: Open a trip: TRAVEL DOCUMENTS lists the passports, ID and health cards in your Vault (a driving licence when a car is booked, and travel insurance) and says whether each lasts the trip; a passport with under six months left when you're back is lit. Open copy shows a kept copy after your fingerprint (Touch ID on the Mac), offline too. Ask "are our passports OK for Barcelona?" for the same answer.
tip: A trip's days count as away: no commute, train alerts or leave-by those days (an evening flight on the first day still lets you work it), Work reads "Work while away", nobody is asked to cover the school days, and the brief and the week ahead say where you are. Book annual leave as well if you won't be working.
tip: Times are as on your tickets: the way there is UK time, everything after it is local. When the trip's name is a place Navilife knows ("Barcelona", "Tenerife"), its row says how far the clocks are apart ("1 h ahead of UK time") and each booking there shows UK time beside it ("18:20 · Flight BA479 · 17:20 UK time").
tip: While you're away on a trip named after a place Navilife knows, the morning brief gives that place's weather instead of home's ("In Barcelona today, 18–24°, clear", in local time), and the day before you go it adds tomorrow's there, so you know what to pack. Only the place's name goes to the weather service, as with home.
tip: Removed a booking or a trip by mistake? Tap Undo on the line that says what went (it stays a few seconds). A week after a trip ends it moves under PAST TRIPS at the bottom: tap it to see your past trips, newest first, each still opening its timeline.
tip: Every trip has Lists: To buy, To pack and Before we leave. Type "buy sun cream" or "pack 2 chargers" and it goes on the right list, in its group (Pharmacy, Documents, Tech…), with "× 2"; anything else goes on the list you have open.
tip: On To pack, "for the kids" or "my …" says whose it is, and Everyone · Me · Kids shows just theirs. A tick shows at once on the Fold and the Mac and sinks to the bottom; Bought and Packed count how far you are. Press and hold an item (right-click on the Mac) to remove it, with Undo.
tip: Anything on a trip's To buy is on your shopping list too, marked "For Barcelona trip". Tick it in either place (in the shop, or on the trip) and both are ticked, on the Fold and the Mac; if it was already on your shopping list, the trip shares it rather than adding it twice.
tip: A new trip with empty lists offers "Start from" your last trip: one tap copies its To buy, To pack and Before we leave, all unticked (Undo takes them off again), and the To buy ones go on your shopping list for the new trip.
tip: Ask or Talk to Obi: "add sun cream to my trip list", "pack the passports for Lisbon" or "what's left to pack?". He shows a card with what goes on which list; tap Add (or say yes) and it is added, Undo takes it off again.
