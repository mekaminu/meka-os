what: Navilife follows one or two Discord channels you pick, from Discord's own notifications on your phone, and shows their posts in Needs you as one card per channel with Done.
how: In Discord set the channel to "All messages" and mute the rest of the server; once a post's notification arrives, the channel is listed under Work mode → Discord: tap Follow.
tip: Only channels you follow are kept; everything else from Discord is ignored.
tip: Navilife never posts to Discord and never trades: a trade idea stays text.
tip: An image shows as "sent an image", a long post may be cut, and nothing from before you followed the channel is there.
tip: Under "Alert me straight away" add people or words (a ticker, "entry", "stop"): a post from one of them, or saying one, shows as a Discord alert notification at once and wears a brass chip on its card.
tip: With Navilife's AI on, a channel with three or more new posts gets a one-line summary at the top of its card ("Admin's BTC long above 68k, stop 66.5k; two charts"). The posts' words go to the AI for that line only; a channel or server named under Messages → "Kept from Navilife's AI" never does.
