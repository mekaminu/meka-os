keywords: spam calls, spam call, spam protection, block a number, block number, blocked calls, blocked numbers, unblock, caller id
fold: WORK
fold_where: Ask → Settings → Work mode → Spam protection
mac: ACTIVITY
mac_where: Ask → Settings → Activity → Blocked calls
---
what: numbers your network couldn't verify, strangers who call twice in a week without a message and strangers who hang up on the call assistant are blocked, so they don't ring.
how: It's always on while Navilife is the phone's Caller ID & spam app. Each block comes with Undo, and Activity → Blocked calls lists them with Unblock.
mac_how: The Mac lists the blocked numbers with Unblock; the blocking happens on the Fold.
tip: Contacts, anyone you called in 90 days and emergency numbers are never blocked.
tip: Allow Navilife to read contacts and the call log, or repeat callers and hang-ups can't be caught.
tip: Android's own Blocked numbers list isn't used: Navilife's screening keeps the call from ringing instead.
