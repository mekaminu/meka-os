what: jobs Obi repeats for you, like a Sunday reset or a payday check: each routine says when it runs, what it covers and when it runs next, with a switch to pause it.
how: Open it from Ask → Daily. Under Start with one, Add a starter (Sunday reset, Morning brief, Study night or Payday check); it appears under Your routines, switched on. Tap a routine's row to pause it or switch it back on, on both the Fold and the Mac; Remove takes it away.
tip: A routine never sends a message, pays, books or trades. Obi only does what Navilife already does by itself, like tidying the inbox; anything else just shows you or asks you.
