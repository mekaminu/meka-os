what: jobs Obi repeats for you, like a Sunday reset or a payday check: each routine says when it runs and what it covers, with a switch to pause it; its page says when it runs next.
how: Open it from Ask → Daily. Under Start with one, Add a starter (Sunday reset, Morning brief, Study night or Payday check); it appears under Your routines, switched on. Its switch pauses it or switches it back on, on both the Fold and the Mac. Remove, on a routine's page, takes it away.
tip: + New makes your own: say or type it in one sentence, like "Every Sunday at 7pm, show my bills for the week, plan next week and tidy my inbox". Obi turns it into steps with when it runs; tap a step's label to change it, then Save routine. Anything that would send a message, pay, book or trade is left out, and it says so.
tip: Tap a routine to open its page: when it runs next, its steps and what each one does. Tap a step's label to change it between Just show, Asks you and Obi does it.
tip: Run now on a routine's page starts it: the Running now card shows the step it's on, with Carry on, Skip this step and Stop, on both apps. Obi does it steps go by on their own, left to Inbox autopilot. Afterwards Last time lists what each step came to.
tip: A routine that's switched on starts by itself at its time: a quiet notice says it has started (in quiet hours it waits for your next digest) and a tap opens Routines with its Running now card. If you already ran it that day, or the phone was off for more than two hours past its time, it waits for its next time.
tip: Ideas from your week, under the list, are routines Obi noticed from what you already do: the evening before your bins go out, the Fridays you tick tasks off late, the evenings you study. Tap one to see it in New routine with its sentence filled in, change anything, then Save routine; once saved it isn't offered again.
tip: A routine never sends a message, pays, books or trades. Obi only does what Navilife already does by itself, like tidying the inbox; anything else just shows you or asks you.
