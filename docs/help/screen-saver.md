what: shows the bedside clock by itself while the phone charges and the screen times out.
how: Take me there opens Android's Screen saver page: pick Navilife bedside clock and choose While charging.
tip: After your quiet hours start it dims right down; a touch goes to the lock screen, nothing on it acts without unlocking.
