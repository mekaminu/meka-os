what: which bins go out, reminded the evening before.
how: Type each bin once with its day, e.g. "Black bin Tue fortnightly from 13 Oct" or "Food caddy every Tuesday"; for every-other-week bins give the date of one collection.
tip: Bank holidays move a collection a day later; type one-offs like "Black bin Sat 2 Jan instead of 29 Dec".
tip: The reminder comes the evening before at 19:00; pick another time under REMINDER (17:00 to 21:00) and both your phone and your Mac remind you then. Today says it from 16:00 either way.
tip: From 16:00 the evening before a collection your Galaxy Watch shows which bins go out tonight, lit from your reminder time; a moved or skipped collection says so. The watch's tile says it too, in place of the inbox count, and goes at midnight.
tip: Use your council's own dates: on its bin-day page, download your bin calendar (.ics), then under COUNCIL'S DATES tap Use the council's dates and pick it. It's read on your phone or Mac and nothing is sent; Christmas weeks follow the council. Use my own days goes back to what you typed.
