what: your lists in one place: Shopping, Waiting for, Someday, Decisions and Renewals (bills, insurance, the car and subscriptions with the day each renews).
how: Open a list and type what to add; tap the ring to tick something off, and Undo puts it back.
tip: Ask can add to them too: "add milk and eggs to shopping".
tip: A renewal with a pay link reminds you three days before with the amount; Navilife never pays anything itself.
tip: A bill's reminder 3 days before says when it costs £1 or more than last time, more or less ("£10 more than last time (£180)"); a credit card's bill that's also a card in Money gets one reminder, the card's.
