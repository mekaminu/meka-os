what: share a screenshot or a photo (a ticket, a booking, a school letter, a parking confirmation) and Navilife reads its words on the device, then offers what to do with them. The picture is never sent or kept.
how: Share → Navilife from Gallery or a screenshot's preview: "Reading the picture on this phone…", then the words fill the capture field. Tap ✦ Handle this for cards, the 🅿 parking timer for a parking confirmation, or Add to keep it as a task. For a paper letter, open Capture and tap 📷 Photo of a letter.
mac_how: Right-click a screenshot or photo in Finder → Services → Handle this picture with Navilife, or use 🖼 Read a picture… in the menu bar's capture, or right-click the mic in Today's capture field → Read the copied picture. The cards wait in Needs you.
tip: Reading happens on the device (Google's ML Kit on the phone, Apple's Vision on the Mac); only the words go on, and only to Navilife's AI when you tap Handle this.
tip: A screenshot's clock, battery and buttons are left out. If it says no words were found, crop the picture to the part that matters and share it again.
tip: The camera's photo of a letter is deleted as soon as it's read and never goes to your gallery.
tip: To have new screenshots noticed by themselves, open Capture and tap "Notice new screenshots" under Photo of a letter and allow Navilife to see your photos (Allow all).
tip: With Notice new screenshots on, each new one is read on the phone; one that looks like a booking, ticket, appointment, order, receipt, letter or parking confirmation gets a quiet "Screenshot · looks like …" notification with Handle this. A chat or a meme says nothing.
tip: On the Mac, turn on Notice new screenshots in the menu bar's capture: a new screen capture that looks like something to act on gets the same notification, read on the Mac.
tip: A photo or screenshot of a passport, driving licence, GHIC, railcard, insurance certificate or warranty offers 🗂 Add to the Vault with its name and the day it runs out (a passport takes the holder's first name, e.g. "Alex passport"). Only the name and date are kept, never its number or the picture. On the Mac the bar offers the same after the cards.
