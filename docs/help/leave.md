what: the leave you've booked, the days left and the school holidays beside them.
how: Tap + Add → A stretch of leave and type it once, e.g. "27–30 Oct" or "Spain 18 Dec – 4 Jan"; only work days count, and on leave days there's no commute or work band.
tip: The three numbers up top are the days you've got left (or how many over), how soon your next leave is and the school holidays still to book.
tip: Tap a school holiday to see how much of it is booked and Book its work days in one tap; tap a stretch to Remove it.
tip: Tap Allowance to change your days a year and the month the leave year starts.
