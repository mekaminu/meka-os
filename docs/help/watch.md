what: Navilife on your Galaxy Watch: Up next with Done, quick capture by voice, a tile and a complication.
how: Open Navilife on the watch and enter the code it shows under Settings → Watch.
tip: The watch syncs on its own, it isn't a remote screen for the phone.
tip: On a Barça match day the watch shows the match from an hour before kick-off (when and where it's on), then the live score and the last goal, then full time; the tile says the same in one line and, while it's on, the complication's ring is the score. Each goal's buzz is the phone's heads-up on your wrist.
tip: Before a meeting with other people the watch shows the prep too, from the lead time you set in Mail until ten minutes after the start: when it starts, who's coming and the one or two most useful lines (their last email, what's open, when you last met); the tile says it in one line.
tip: With no signal the watch keeps what you tap (Done, captures, a fast) and sends it when it's back online; the foot of its screen says "Offline · your changes here wait to sync" and the tile says "Waiting to sync" until they've gone.
tip: To unlink the watch from the watch itself, scroll to the foot of its screen and tap Unlink this watch, then Unlink: it needs a connection, sends what's waiting first, then forgets your day and shows a new code. You can also unlink it from Settings → Watch on the phone or the Mac.
