what: an evening every two weeks, kept clear of plans and gym bookings.
how: Tap + Add → The evening and pick the weekday, the time and which week it starts; a week before Navilife asks you to book somewhere and arrange cover.
tip: The three numbers up top are how soon the next one is, whether it's booked and the evening's time.
tip: Tap a night under Coming up to mark it Booked or Skip this one; a skipped evening is free to plan again.
tip: Tap The evening to change the day or time, or Turn off.
