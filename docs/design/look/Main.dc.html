The Navilife look

One design language for every screen on the Fold, the watch and the Mac. Money's approved redesign is the starting point; this page takes what works there and makes it the rule everywhere. Nothing on screen changes until you say yes.

Dark is the default Light looks right too

Six rules

1 · One brass thing per screen
Brass marks the single thing to do next: the main button, today, progress. Everything else is grey on black.
2 · Numbers, not sentences
"£184 · 6 days left" beats a line of prose. A sentence only when Obi is talking to you.
3 · Cards for everything
Every block is one card: a small label, a figure, up to 5 rows, then "See all". No floating text between cards.
4 · Nothing open until tapped
No open fields or chip rows on a screen. A row says what's set; a tap opens a sheet to change it.
5 · Help behind ⓘ, said once
Explanations sit behind a small ⓘ. A disclaimer appears once, at the foot of the screen, never on every card.
6 · One skeleton for every tab
Title · one line of state · tiles · pills · cards · foot. Learn one screen and you know them all.

Icons · one line weight

Outline, 1.75 line, rounded ends, 24 box (20 inside rows). Grey when quiet; filled brass only when it's the tab you're on. No emoji anywhere in the app.
